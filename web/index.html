How do I get it onto my iPhone?

Both files have to arrive together, through a route that understands Live Photos:

  • Works On a Mac: drag both files into the Photos app at the same time. With iCloud Photos on, it syncs to your iPhone. You can also AirDrop it from Photos.
  • Doesn't work Uploading on icloud.com, or the iCloud for Windows upload folder: they split it into a separate photo and video.
  • On the iPhone itself, try Share / Save… and then Save 2 Items. This route is experimental.

The LIVE badge can show up before the video part has downloaded to your phone. If it doesn't move yet, give it a few minutes.
